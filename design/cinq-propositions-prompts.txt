1. NOËL NÉON
Fichier : /Volumes/EXT/Dev/pianoTiles/design/proposition-01-noel-neon.png

PROMPT
Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high fidelity graphic proposal for a mobile PWA piano-tiles rhythm game competition between office colleagues for CHRISTMAS. Christmas must be immediately and unmistakably visible in EVERY concept, while the music is normal pop/electronic/piano, never Christmas songs. App provisional name "tempo". French text. Premium art direction. Landscape board, three large equal-height perfectly front-facing portrait smartphone mockups side by side, no perspective. Screens occupy most of canvas height. Thin elegant frames, fully visible screens, no hands, no photographic backdrop. Board heading at top with the specified concept number and title. Three screens: left home, center actual four-lane falling-tile gameplay, right colleague leaderboard. All typography legible, clear touch targets and coherent design language throughout. Home: logo tempo, small "CHALLENGE DE NOËL", bold short headline, striking Christmas graphic hero integrating piano keys, compact "24 collègues dans la course", "Ton record 2 450 pts", clear wide "Jouer" CTA, three bottom tabs Jouer / Classement / Profil. Gameplay: pause button, big score 1 280, timer 00:42, COMBO ×12, four vertical lanes with staggered long rectangular falling piano tiles, clear horizontal hit line and four touch keys near bottom. Keep the gameplay clear, Christmas decorations only in header and margins, never blocking notes. Small track footer "Pop Session". Leaderboard: title "Le classement", small "Édition de Noël", top three named Léa 4 820, Mehdi 4 560, Julie 4 310, in correct rank order with center first if podium; then Thomas 3 980 and Emma 3 740, highlighted personal row "8  Toi  2 450". Christmas gift reward detail. Matching bottom nav. Names and scores are fictional. Overall visually compelling, beautifully composed, designed like a real polished mobile app by a senior art director. No childish 3D piano mascot, no giant comic battle starbursts, no racing checkerboards, no recycled SaaS cards.
ART DIRECTION 01 — NOËL NÉON. Heading exactly "01 / NOËL NÉON". A spectacular Christmas night, deep midnight navy and emerald, luminous cherry red and warm gold neon, subtle mint light. Sophisticated festive music festival aesthetic for adults. Graphic line-art Christmas tree made of glowing piano keys as home hero, tiny neon baubles hanging from a thin cable across header, neon ribbon wrapping gift beneath tree. Strong clean geometric grotesk typography, restrained bright outlines, richly dark backgrounds, high contrast white text. Headline "Fais briller ton score." Large red glowing CTA with white text. Game tiles feel like luminous glass piano keys red, mint, warm white, subtly reflected, clean precision. Rank screen trophy rendered as a sculptural luminous golden star on emerald plinth with subtle Christmas light strings, colleague initials avatars, no cartoon faces. Dark midnight board with small golden stars sparingly at outer margins. Christmas clearly recognizable, electric and classy, NOT generic cyberpunk.
